MolCraft Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · metalemma

MolCraft is a virtual-reality game for Meta Quest in which you build molecules from atoms. This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: MolCraft has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

Information stored on your headset

The game saves your progress on the headset itself: the molecules in your world, DNA you have assembled, challenge progress, unlocked structures, your language choice and other settings, and a cache of compound names it has looked up. This data never leaves the device through MolCraft. You can erase it from the game's settings panel (the reset option) or by uninstalling the game.

Compound name lookup

When you build a structure whose name is not in the game's built-in list, MolCraft asks public chemistry databases for its name. The request contains only a standard chemical identifier of the structure (its InChIKey), and is sent to:

  • PubChem, run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (privacy policy), and
  • Wikidata, run by the Wikimedia Foundation (privacy policy).

As with any internet request, these services receive your device's IP address and the name of the app making the request. MolCraft sends no other information about you or your device. If the headset is offline, the lookup simply does not happen and the game works as normal.

Meta platform

MolCraft runs on Meta Horizon OS. When the game starts, it asks Meta's platform to confirm that your account owns the game. Controller and hand tracking are provided by the headset and used by the game only in real time to show and move your hands; MolCraft does not record or transmit them. Information Meta collects through its platform is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Children

MolCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed.
